# FSSAI FoSCoS

## Source

Organization: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

Document Title: Food Business Services / FoSCoS

Official URL: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/

Source Type: Government Digital Service

## Content

FSSAI provides food business services through the Food Safety Compliance System (FoSCoS).

The platform provides services for applying for a new food licence or registration.

The platform also provides facilities for tracking applications, renewing registrations or licences, modifying existing applications and managing food business compliance.

The FSSAI food business service portal provides access to licensing and registration services and related guidance documents.

The FoSCoS system is the pan-India platform used for food business licensing and registration.
