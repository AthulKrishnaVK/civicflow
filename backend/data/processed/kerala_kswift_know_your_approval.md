# Kerala K-SWIFT Know Your Approval

## Source

Organization: Kerala State Industrial Development Corporation / Government of Kerala

Document Title: Know Your Approval

Official URL: https://kswift.kerala.gov.in/index/kya_qusetionaire.php

Source Type: Government Approval Discovery Service

## Content

K-SWIFT provides a Know Your Approval information wizard to identify compliances applicable to establishing a business.

The system provides information about licenses, registrations and No Objection Certificates required at pre-establishment and pre-operation stages.

The system can provide information about fees, procedural details, documentation required, timelines for receiving services, application links, application tracking and final approvals or certificates.

The questionnaire considers factors such as area of operation, size of firm, type of operation, ownership, land-related information, environmental considerations and whether the business is a Food Business Operator.

The Food Business Operator question covers businesses that handle, prepare, manufacture, transport, repack, store, process or market food, as well as retailers, wholesalers and suppliers of food items.
